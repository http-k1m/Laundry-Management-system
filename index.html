Welcome

Qwetu Laundry takes the laundry day off your timetable. Put your clothes in a tagged bag, hand it over on your floor's pickup day, and collect it clean, dry and folded two days later.

Drop and go

No queues at the machines. Leave your bag at the laundry bay on your floor before 7:00 am.

Every bag tracked

Each bag gets a numbered tag, so you always know whether it is washing, drying or ready.

Student prices

A full wash and dry starts at KES 250 a load, and you can pay by M-Pesa or on collection.

How it works

  1. Book your wash on the booking page.
  2. Pack your clothes in the bag and write your tag number on the form.
  3. Hand the bag over on your floor's pickup day (see the schedule).
  4. Collect it from the laundry desk when you get the ready message.

Laundry desk hours

  • Monday to Saturday: 7:00 am to 7:00 pm
  • Sunday: 10:00 am to 4:00 pm

Lost a sock?

Call the helpline on 0700 000 111 within 7 days and quote your bag tag number.
