Questions students ask

How do I pay?

Pay by M-Pesa when you book, or at the laundry desk when you collect your bag.

What if my clothes shrink or fade?

Tell us about delicate items in the care notes. If we damage something, report it at the desk within 48 hours and we will compensate you.

Can someone else collect my bag?

Yes, if they bring your tag number and your student ID number.

Do you wash shoes or school uniforms?

We wash uniforms and sports kit. We do not wash shoes or leather.

How do I know my order is ready?

You will get an SMS with your tag number. You can also ask at the desk.

Laundry desk hours

  • Monday to Saturday: 7:00 am to 7:00 pm
  • Sunday: 10:00 am to 4:00 pm

Lost a sock?

Call the helpline on 0700 000 111 within 7 days and quote your bag tag number.
