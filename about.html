Who we are

Qwetu Laundry started because of a simple problem: a hostel of hundreds of students and only a handful of washing machines. Students lost whole Saturdays waiting in line, and clothes went missing from shared dryers.

We began with four machines and a notebook. Every bag now has a number, and every number has a name.

What we promise

Your clothes stay yours

Bags are sealed, tagged and checked in and out by two staff.

Gentle on fabric

Colours and whites are washed apart, and delicates get a cool cycle.

On time

Standard orders are back within 48 hours. Express orders are back the same day.

Laundry desk hours

  • Monday to Saturday: 7:00 am to 7:00 pm
  • Sunday: 10:00 am to 4:00 pm

Lost a sock?
