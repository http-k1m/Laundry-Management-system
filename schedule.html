Pickup and return days

Each wing has two pickup days a week. Leave your bag at your floor's laundry bay before 7:00 am. Standard orders come back two days later.

Good to know

  • Bags not collected within 14 days are donated to a local charity.
  • Check pockets before you pack. We cannot be responsible for money or keys left inside.
  • Public holidays follow the Sunday hours.

Laundry desk hours

  • Monday to Saturday: 7:00 am to 7:00 pm
  • Sunday: 10:00 am to 4:00 pm

Lost a sock?

Call the helpline on 0700 000 111 within 7 days and quote your bag tag number.
